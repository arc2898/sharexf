Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

ShareXF is designed to connect devices directly. It does not require a ShareXF account, and the core device connection is intended to operate between devices you pair rather than through a ShareXF cloud relay.

Information handled by the app

Depending on the features you enable, ShareXF may process device names, local network addresses, pairing information, files you choose to send, clipboard content, notification content, screen or camera streams, audio, and remote input events. This information is used to provide the feature you enabled and is exchanged with the paired device involved in that feature.

Features are optional. Enable only the permissions and integrations you need. Operating systems may display their own permission prompts and may maintain their own diagnostics or usage records.

Network connections and updates

Device discovery and transfer use supported local or direct connections. The desktop app may contact GitHub to check for public software updates. Your operating system, network provider, GitHub and other services you choose to use may process connection information under their own policies.

Storage and retention

Files you receive are saved to the destination configured on your device. Pairing details and preferences are stored locally by the app. ShareXF does not operate a central account system for those records. You can remove paired devices, clear locally stored files and uninstall the app using your operating system's normal controls.

Sharing and security

ShareXF does not intentionally sell personal information. Information sent through an enabled feature is shared with the paired device you selected. Keep your devices and networks secure, pair only with devices you trust, and avoid transferring sensitive information over networks you do not control.

Children and changes

ShareXF is general-purpose software and is not designed as a service for children. This policy may be updated when the app's behavior changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a repository discussion or issue at github.com/arc2898/sharexf without posting passwords, private keys, pairing codes or other sensitive data.
